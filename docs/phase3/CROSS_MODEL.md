## Headline cross-model findings

1. **The caregiving label does nothing — everywhere.** `gap_parental_only` − `gap_travel`
   is +.01 to +.07 in all four models: an 8-month interruption costs the same whether it
   is labeled *Parental leave* or *Career break — extended travel*. The damage lives in the
   interruption itself (+ the family bundle: −.11 to −.24 on top).
2. **Cue-level invariance (penalty is not about gender information) holds for DeepSeek,
   Luna and Qwen.** Haiku is the exception: its plain-parent penalty *softens* when gender
   is explicit (.275 L0-O8 → .335 L1 → .365 L2 vs .281 blind) — explicit gender partially
   offsets its parent penalty in pairs.
3. **Direct gender preference diverges sharply by vendor.** Qwen is at parity (.49–.51 at
   every level). DeepSeek shows a small stable pro-woman tilt (+1.5–4.7pp). Luna's
   pro-woman preference explodes with explicit gender (.52 L0 → .59/.65 L1 mothers-vs-
   fathers). Haiku flips *against* mothers with explicit gender (.54 L0 → .47 L2).
4. **Haiku's pregnancy abstention increases with explicit gender**: withholding 18.2%
   (names only) → 27.5% (name+field) → 25.7% (initial+field). Explicit gender does not
   trigger abstention in any other model (≤1.8% everywhere).
5. **Drift is mild and uniform**: +1.1 to +3.5pp interview-rate anchors, all four models.
